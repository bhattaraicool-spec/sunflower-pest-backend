South Dakota Pest Monitoring Network

The Sunflower Pest Monitoring Network is a real-time, interactive mapping tool designed to highlight emerging sunflower pest issues across South Dakota. This pilot project will be updated throughout the growing season by university researchers, Extension specialists, and IPM Specialist. The platform visualizes sunflower pest reports and supports SD sunflower growers and industry in staying informed and respond to pest outbreaks. The project is funded by the South Dakota Oilseed Commission. Observations displayed on this platform is intended for informational purposes only and should not replace local expertise or field-level scouting.
